Why MBFC + SH?

Four Questions Became Four Books.

Years of operational work kept returning to four connected questions. Together they became the MBFC + SH series.

01 · Improvement

What actually constitutes improvement when a decision creates benefits in one place and friction somewhere else?

02 · Judgment

How can people make better decisions when information is incomplete and the outcome is still unknown?

03 · Organizations

Why do capable people make locally reasonable decisions that can produce poor organizational results?

04 · Capability

How can leaders build judgment, ownership, and capability rather than making themselves the destination for every difficult decision?

“The best leaders do not make themselves irrelevant. They make their organizations more capable.”

The MBFC + SH Series

The series begins with a framework for defining improvement, moves into judgment under uncertainty, examines the organizational conditions that shape decisions, and concludes with leadership capability.
