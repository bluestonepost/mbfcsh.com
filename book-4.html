Capability in Practice

JudgmentDevelop the ability to make sound decisions.
OwnershipMove responsibility toward the people closest to the work.
AccountabilityMake expectations and consequences clear.
SystemsBuild mechanisms that support repeatable performance.
LearningUse experience to improve future decisions.
CapabilityCreate strength that remains after the leader steps away.
