Every decision moves an organization somewhere.

The question is whether it creates net improvement.

MBFC + SH gives organizations a common language for evaluating what a decision creates and what friction it introduces. The objective is not isolated improvement. The objective is a better organization as a whole.

A Question Worth Asking

What’s the cost of this decision?

The visible price is rarely the entire consequence. Better decisions consider value, friction, tradeoffs, duration, significance, and effects across the whole system.

This question connects the practical examples throughout MBFC + SH with the deeper progression of the four books.
