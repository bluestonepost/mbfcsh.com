The Improvement Compass

A Framework for Better Decisions and Improvement

Value creation is only half the question. The framework also examines waste, defects, delays, excess cost, risk, and frustration before weighing tradeoffs and determining net improvement.

The Core Question
Does this decision create net improvement for the organization as a whole?
A Recurring Six-Part Language

Framework in Practice

MoreIncrease useful output, capacity, reach, or value.
BetterImprove quality, effectiveness, reliability, or experience.
FasterReduce time, delay, waiting, or unnecessary motion.
CheaperReduce total cost without shifting hidden costs elsewhere.
SaferReduce risk to people, customers, assets, and the organization.
HappierReduce avoidable frustration and improve the human experience.
