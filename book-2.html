A Recurring Six-Part Language

Judgment in Practice

PerspectiveLook at the decision from more than one point of view.
AssumptionsMake hidden assumptions visible before they harden into facts.
EvidenceSeparate what is known from what is believed or inferred.
UncertaintyRecognize what cannot yet be known.
TradeoffsExamine what is gained, lost, shifted, or delayed.
LearningImprove the quality of thinking regardless of the eventual outcome.
