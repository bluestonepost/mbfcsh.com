Four Books · Four Applications

A Progression Organizations Can Use

Each book can support a focused discussion. Together they move from defining improvement to building lasting organizational capability.

01 · FrameworkThe Improvement Compass

Establish a common language for improvement and examine whether decisions create net improvement.

 02 · JudgmentThe Decision Lens

Strengthen judgment by examining perspectives, assumptions, alternatives, tradeoffs, risks, and consequences.

 03 · OrganizationsDecision Architecture

Examine the organizational conditions that shape how decisions are made and how the organization responds.

 04 · CapabilityLeadership Leverage

Build judgment, ownership, accountability, and capability rather than dependence on the leader.
Start With a Real Question

Six Questions to Bring Into the Room

MBFC + SH works best when the discussion begins with something the organization is already trying to understand, decide, or improve.

01 · Value
What are we actually trying to improve?
02 · Friction
What friction could this decision create or reduce?
03 · Judgment
What assumptions, alternatives, and tradeoffs are we missing?
04 · Conditions
What organizational conditions are shaping this decision?
05 · Capability
Will this leave the organization more capable next time?
06 · Learning
What will tell us whether the decision actually worked?
Facilitation & Organizational Support

Use the Materials Independently—or With Facilitation.

The books, case studies, and resources are designed to be useful without outside facilitation. Organizations that want help applying the ideas to a specific challenge can also inquire about facilitated discussions, workshops, training sessions, or other organizational uses.
